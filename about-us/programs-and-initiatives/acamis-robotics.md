# ACAMIS Robotics

ACAMIS (Association of China and Mongolia International Schools) is an activities and athletics organization for international schools, hosting regional tournaments and the ACAMIS National Championship which qualifies one team for the VEX World Championship. Offering both the VEX IQ Robotics Competition, VEX V5 Robotics Competition and First Lego League competitions, Robotics at ACAMIS is led by a committee of teachers and faculty from ACAMIS schools throughout China.

For the purposes of qualification, ACAMIS Robotics is divided into three regions: North, Central and South. Teams associated with ACAMIS schools compete in ACAMIS Regional Tournaments or other tournaments hosted by various international schools across China to qualify for the ACAMIS National Championship in late February.&#x20;

For more information, please see the following link: [https://www.acamis.org/student-activities/robotics](https://www.acamis.org/student-activities/robotics).
