The ISB HS Robotics Club hosted a V5RC scrimmage in Gym 1, with participating teams from the International School of Beijing, Beijing City International School, Daystar Academy, HD School Beijing and Beanstalk International Bilingual School.

Congratulations to 34844B and 86X for becoming tournament champions!

Please contact Patrick Young (hellohelloju1@gmail.com) for further information inquiries about the ISB Robotics Scrimmage in 2025.
