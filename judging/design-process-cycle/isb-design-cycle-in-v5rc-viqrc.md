# ISB Design Cycle in V5RC/VIQRC

At ISB, the design curriculum is based upon the ISB Design Cycle which loosely incorporates the stages of the VEX Engineering Design Process, or the simplified Engineering Design Process. While most teams in VEX competitions have used the design process as listed in the Engineering Notebook Rubric, teams at ISB have traditionally used our own ISB Design Cycle for the purposes of the notebook-making process, which is adequate for robot design documentation but can lack rather severely in some areas of the rubric.&#x20;
