# Awards, Guides and Judging Q\&A

[The Guide to Judging](https://kb.roboticseducation.org/hc/en-us/articles/4788499711127-Guide-to-Judging-Introduction-and-Terms) is the authoritative document for the judging process in all V5RC and VIQRC tournaments, with the Judging Q\&A and non-judging specific RECF policies also providing procedural guidance and regulations for judging.&#x20;

The Judging Q\&A is available via Robotevents, which allows the Competition Judging Committee (CJC), the RECF body overseeing judging at RECF events, to ask for clarification related to the Guide to Judging or the judging process.&#x20;

> The Q\&A is only accessible by Event Partners or the Primary, Secondary or Financial contact of a team. For ISB Robotics team members, please get in touch with your coach to ask for a Q\&A.
